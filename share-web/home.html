FAQ

Questions, answered

Is this a clinic?

No — it’s your personal aesthetic journey. Care stays between you and your providers.

Who sees my photos?

Private by default. You choose what to share via dedicated links — or make a post live when you’re ready. Nothing is public unless you decide.

What happens to my data when I delete it?

Deleted means deleted forever. Your photos, notes, and timeline entries are permanently removed from our servers.

Is it only for the face?

No — face and body. Log any procedure, invasive or non-invasive, wherever it belongs on your journey.

Surgical and non-surgical?

Yes. Injectables, skin treatments, body procedures, surgery — invasive and non-invasive — whatever belongs on your timeline.

How do I start?

Get the app, create your account, and tap Get Started.
